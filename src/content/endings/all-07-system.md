No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a phrase written on a card. She set it in the middle of the table where everyone could read it.

WITHIN TOLERANCE.

'I've heard this from every one of you,' she said. 'Separately, in different words, about different things. Marcus: the override was routine. Elias: the variance was within spec. Priya: the risk was within appetite. Jonah: it passed. Renée: the reading was within the threshold.' She tapped the card. 'You all said the same thing. Each about your own piece. Each of you certified that your part was within tolerance.'

The room was quiet.

'And here's what I finally understood,' she said. 'You were all telling the truth. Every single part was within tolerance. The override—within the rules. The variance—within spec. The schedule—within authority. The maintenance—within the window. The reading—within the threshold.' She looked up. 'Every part passed. The man died anyway. Because nothing checks the sum.'

No one spoke.

'A tolerance is a budget for error,' Ellery said. 'A little slack, allowed, on each part. The override gets a little slack. The calibration gets a little slack. The schedule, the check, the threshold—each gets its little allowed margin of wrong. And every one of you spent your whole margin. Right up to the line. Within tolerance. Legally, defensibly, to the letter.' She paused. 'And the slacks stacked. Five little allowed errors, each one fine alone, all pointing the same direction at the same moment. The sum was so far outside tolerance that a man died. But the sum was never measured, because nobody owns the sum. You each owned your part, and your part was fine.'

'Then the tolerances were set wrong,' Priya said. 'That's a design problem, not a—'

'The tolerances were set fine,' Ellery said. 'Each one was reasonable for its part. The mistake isn't in any single tolerance. The mistake is believing that if every part is within tolerance, the whole is too. It isn't. It never was. You can pass every individual check and still build a death, if all the slack leans the same way.' She paused. 'And all your slack leaned the same way, because you were all leaning toward the same morning. The milestone. The schedule. The thing that wanted to happen.'

The room was very still.

'No one exceeded their tolerance,' she said. 'That's going to be every one of your defenses, and every one of them is true. "I was within tolerance." You all were. The phrase is going to be carved on this whole disaster.' She picked up the card. 'It's the most honest thing in the building and it killed him. Within tolerance. Within tolerance. Within tolerance. Five times, leaning together, until the man in the middle was the only thing outside it.'

'The report,' she said, 'will examine each decision against its own tolerance, and find that each one passed. And then it will examine the sum, which no procedure in this building was responsible for examining. It will be the first time anyone added them up.'

She put the card in her pocket.

'You built a place where everyone could be right and a man could still die,' she said. 'And then you all were. And he did.'

She left.

The table was empty where the card had been.
