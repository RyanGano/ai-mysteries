No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery laid out three pieces of evidence: a firmware revision, an access override, and a bypass jumper.

'Three changes to one suit and one room,' she said. 'Elias—you remapped a sensor channel, so the system misread where pressure landed. Jonah—you left a bypass jumper across the interlock, so it couldn't halt. Marcus—you overrode the bay door, so Michael could walk in mid-sequence.' She looked between them. 'Wrong map. Dead interlock. Open door.'

The three of them looked at the objects and at each other.

'The door let him in,' Ellery said. 'The interlock should have stopped the discharge—but Jonah's jumper had bridged it. The sensor mapping should have registered the contact correctly—but Elias's remap had it reading the wrong geometry. So a man walks through a door that should have been locked, into a suit that can't halt, wearing sensors that don't know where he is.'

'I overrode the door at a request,' Rourke said. 'Doors get overridden.'

'I forgot to pull the jumper,' Jonah said. 'It was the window, I was rushing—'

'And I resolved a documentation conflict,' Varga said.

'Three small things,' Ellery said. 'A logged override. A forgotten wire. A firmware fix. Not one of you did anything that, by itself, looks like more than a Tuesday. And together they are a locked room with the lock removed, the alarm cut, and the lights off.' She paused. 'You're a scientist, a technician, and a security officer. You barely overlap. And the places where you don't overlap are exactly the seams he fell through.'

The room was very still.

'Pull any one back,' Ellery said. 'Marcus keeps the door locked—he never gets in. Jonah pulls the jumper—the interlock halts. Elias verifies the mapping against the hardware—the contact reads true and the system stops. Three independent rescues, any one sufficient, none performed.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the remapped sensor channel, the installed bypass jumper, and the door override, and that the death required all three.'

She gathered the three pieces.

'A wrong map, a dead interlock, an open door,' she said. 'One each. He walked through all three of you without ever knowing you were there.'

She left.
