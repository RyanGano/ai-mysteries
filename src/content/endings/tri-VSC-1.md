No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a warning, a reframe, and a silence.

'Renée—you raised the interlock as a hazard, ten days out. Priya—you reframed it as "risk appetite" and recommended proceeding. Elias—you'd run a simulation that found a fatal case, and you filed it in drafts.' She set them down. 'A warning raised, a warning reframed, and a warning never sent. Three things that should have stopped this, each neutralized a different way.'

The three were silent.

'Renée, you saw it from the safety side. Elias, you saw it from the math side. Two independent, correct warnings.' Ellery turned to Priya. 'And you, Priya—when Renée's reached you, you converted it from a hazard into an accepted risk. You didn't get Elias's, because he never sent it. So one warning was talked into silence and the other was kept in silence.'

'I made a documented risk decision,' Priya said.

'My findings were preliminary,' Varga said.

'I had raised mine through channels,' Renée said.

'Look at what each silence did,' Ellery said. 'Elias, if you'd sent your simulation, it would have corroborated Renée—two warnings are harder to reframe than one. Renée, if you'd pushed harder, refused the reframe, your hazard stays a hazard. Priya, your reframe only worked because Renée's warning stood alone; with Elias's number behind it, "risk appetite" doesn't hold.' She paused. 'You each needed the other two to play their part. Elias's silence isolated Renée's warning. That isolation let Priya reframe it. The reframe let everyone proceed.'

The room was very quiet.

'Two of you were right,' Ellery said. 'Elias, Renée—you were right, separately, in detail, in advance. That should be the story: the two who saw it coming. But Elias kept his sight in a drawer, and Renée let hers be relabeled, and Priya did the relabeling, and between the three of you, two correct predictions of a man's death produced exactly nothing.'

'The report,' she said, 'will include the safety warning, the reframing reply, and the unsent simulation, and note that two independent correct warnings existed and that each was neutralized.'

She stood.

'Two of you knew,' she said. 'One of you made sure it didn't matter. And the one who made sure could only do it because the other two stayed quiet in their different ways.'

She left the three records on the table.
