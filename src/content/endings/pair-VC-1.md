No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery looked at the two of them and almost sighed.

'You're the cautious ones,' she said. 'That's what makes this hard. Elias, you've spent two years saying the program is reckless. Renée, you've spent fifteen seeing disasters before they happen. If I had to name the two people in this building most likely to prevent a death, it would be you two.' She paused. 'And you're the two I'm here about.'

Neither of them flinched. They were too alike for that.

'Elias,' she said, 'you narrowed the discharge threshold. Sound engineering, you'd argue. Renée, you widened the alert threshold on the monitoring system. Sound safety practice, you'd argue—too many false alarms. One of you tightened the line that decides "dangerous." One of you loosened the line that decides "alert."' She set the two configs down. 'Between you, you moved both lines just far enough that the fatal reading was neither dangerous enough to halt nor alarming enough to warn.'

'My change reduced nuisance escalations,' Renée said. 'It made the alerts trustworthy.'

'And mine corrected an over-conservative margin,' Varga said.

'I know,' Ellery said. 'You're the two most careful people here, and you each made a careful, defensible adjustment, and your two careful adjustments happened to open a window between them. The reading fell in the window. Not dangerous, by Elias's line. Not alarming, by Renée's. Safe, by both of your careful judgments, simultaneously.'

The room was quiet.

'You're natural allies,' Ellery said. 'You both believe in caution. And I think that's exactly why this slipped past you. You each trusted the other to be the careful one. Elias, you narrowed your threshold partly because you knew Renée's monitoring would catch anything you missed. Renée, you widened your alerts partly because you trusted the engineering margins to hold the line. Each of you leaned on the other's caution to justify a little less of your own.'

'That's speculative,' Varga said.

'It is,' Ellery agreed. 'But here's what isn't. Two careful people, two careful changes, and a man in the gap between them. The recklessness you both fear—it didn't come from the reckless people this time. It came from the two of you, each trusting the other to be the backstop, each becoming a little less of one.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the narrowed discharge threshold and the widened alert threshold, that each was independently defensible, and that the fatal reading fell between them.'

She stood.

'You warned everyone about the cowboys,' she said. 'And the man died in the space your two carefulnesses left between them. Be less careful about trusting each other's care. That's the only lesson I have, and it's a strange one.'

She left.
