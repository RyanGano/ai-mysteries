No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had four findings and one name held back.

'Elias, Priya, Jonah, Renée,' she said. 'The four of you. The one I'm not naming today is Marcus.' She paused. 'Because the four of you are the lab—the science, the schedule, the bench, the safety. Marcus is the building around it. And what killed Michael Holloway came from inside the lab. The four of you are the lab.'

The four were silent.

'Elias—the calibration. Priya—the schedule. Jonah—the verification. Renée—the halt.' She set them down. 'The four functions that actually run a test. Design it, schedule it, prepare it, watch it. Each function failed, in the hands of the person who owns it. This wasn't a break-in or an outside force. The test killed the man who came to watch it, and the test is the four of you.'

'Marcus controls access,' Priya said. 'If we're assigning—'

'Marcus opened a door,' Ellery said. 'A real failure; he'll answer for it. But a door is just a door. Whether the room beyond it kills you depends on the four of you. Marcus let Michael in. The four of you made the inside lethal. You can't hand this to the man who works the perimeter. The danger was internal, and you're the internal.'

The room was very quiet.

'I want to name what the four of you have in common,' Ellery said. 'You're the experts. The deep ones. Marcus knows badges and cameras; he'll tell you himself he doesn't understand the suit. But you four understand the suit completely. Elias built its logic. Priya drove its program. Jonah maintained its body. Renée audited its safety. The four people who understood the machine best are the four whose decisions made it kill. The understanding didn't save him. The understanding is what was in the room when he died.'

'My findings were preliminary,' Varga said.

'I set a schedule,' Priya said.

'I ran out of time,' Jonah said.

'I was overruled before,' Renée said.

'Four experts,' Ellery said. 'Four defenses. And a man who trusted that four experts couldn't all be wrong at once.' She paused. 'He was wrong about that. You were the proof.'

'The report,' she said, 'will name the four of you and your four decisions, and note that the lethal conditions were created entirely within the test's own functions.'

She stood.

'The building let him in,' she said. 'The lab killed him. You four are the lab.'

She left the four findings on the table.
