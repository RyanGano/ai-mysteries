No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a suppressed presence layer, a must-run flag, and an un-invoked halt.

'Marcus—you suppressed the proximity-confirmation layer, so the room couldn't confirm a person. Priya—you flagged the test must-run, foreclosing the review. Renée—you had the halt authority and the doubt, and didn't use it.' She set them down. 'No eyes, no pause, no stop.'

The three were silent.

'These are the three ways a test gets stopped,' Ellery said. 'The system detects a person and halts automatically—Marcus removed that. A human review questions the run before it starts—Priya removed that. The safety officer pulls the handle—Renée didn't pull it. Automatic, procedural, manual. The three brakes on this whole operation, and all three were off.'

'My flag was routine,' Rourke said.

'I prioritized within my authority,' Priya said.

'I had been overruled before,' Renée said.

'Renée, this one's hardest on you,' Ellery said, 'because you were the last brake. The automatic one was already gone—Marcus's flag. The procedural one was already gone—Priya's must-run. You were what was left. The whole safety of that morning had collapsed down onto one person's willingness to pull a handle. And you'd learned, from Priya's flags and a hundred overrulings, that pulling it was futile. So you didn't.'

The room was very quiet.

'You'll say the handle wouldn't have worked,' Ellery said. 'Against a must-run flag, against Priya, against the board. Maybe. But you didn't test it. And here's what makes it three people and not one: Marcus's suppression and Priya's flag are exactly what taught you the handle was futile. They built the environment in which a safety officer stops believing in her own authority. The other two brakes failing is why the third brake didn't even try.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the suppressed presence layer, the must-run designation, and the un-invoked halt, and that they represent the failure of the automatic, procedural, and manual means of stopping the test.'

She stood.

'Three brakes,' she said. 'One blinded, one forbidden, one that had given up. By the time it came down to the last one, the first two had already convinced her there was no point.'

She left the three records on the table.
