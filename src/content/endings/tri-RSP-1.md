No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had an access grant, a compressed schedule, and a skipped check.

'Marcus—you gave Michael deep-tier access, so his badge opened the bay. Priya—you compressed the window and flagged the test must-run. Jonah—in the time you had left, you skipped the verification.' She set them down. 'The door that let him in. The schedule that rushed the work. The check that wasn't done.'

The three were silent.

'Marcus put him in the room,' Ellery said. 'Priya made sure the suit wasn't properly prepared—not directly, but by taking the time for it. Jonah dropped the step that would have caught the fault. A man with access he shouldn't have, wearing a suit checked too fast, in a room cleared to run no matter what.'

'I granted access at his request,' Rourke said.

'I set a schedule,' Priya said.

'I did what the window allowed,' Jonah said.

'There's a pressure line through all three of you,' Ellery said. 'Priya's must-run flag is the engine. Marcus, you smoothed the founder's path into the room because the demo mattered and Michael wanted to be there for it. Jonah, you cut the check because Priya's window left no room. The same milestone pulled all three levers—the access, the schedule, the shortcut. None of you set it in motion. You each just did the accommodating thing in your own lane.'

The room was very quiet.

'Pull one back,' Ellery said. 'Marcus keeps the access tight—Michael watches from behind the glass, lives. Priya gives the full window—Jonah does the check, the fault is caught. Jonah does the check despite the rush—same. Three rescues, any one enough. Three accommodations instead, all toward the same morning.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the deep-tier access grant, the compressed must-run schedule, and the skipped verification, and that together they placed an inadequately verified suit on a man who should not have been in the bay.'

She stood.

'You let him in, you rushed the prep, you skipped the step,' she said. 'One each, all for the demo, none of you talking. He walked your smoothed path into a suit nobody finished checking.'

She left the three records on the table.
