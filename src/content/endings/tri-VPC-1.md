No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a substitute mesh, a filtered reading, and a widened threshold.

'Three layers, one leak,' she said. 'Jonah—you repaired the grounding mesh with non-spec material, so a fault could put current where it shouldn't go. Elias—your model filtered the resulting anomalous reading as noise. Renée—your widened threshold meant the reading never escalated anyway.' She set them down. 'The leak was made, then dismissed twice—once by the model, once by the threshold.'

The three were silent.

'It's a defense in depth, run backwards,' Ellery said. 'Normally you'd have three lines catching a fault: the part holds, the model flags it, the monitor alarms. Here, the part didn't hold—Jonah's substitution. So it fell to the model—which Elias's filter dismissed as an outlier. So it fell to the monitor—which Renée's threshold had tuned past noticing. Three layers of protection, three failures, perfectly nested.'

'I was out of the spec mesh,' Jonah said.

'The reading was a statistical outlier,' Varga said.

'I retuned to suppress false alarms,' Renée said.

'And here's why the nesting is so tight,' Ellery said. 'Jonah's substitution created a fault no one expected on that unit. Elias, your model filtered it precisely because it was unexpected—an implausible reading from a sound part. Renée, your threshold let it pass because it started small, building slowly the way a degraded ground does. The unexpectedness defeated the model. The slowness defeated the monitor. The substitution caused both.'

The room was very quiet.

'Defense in depth is supposed to mean that no single failure is fatal,' Ellery said. 'Three layers, so one can fail and the others catch it. But the layers aren't independent here. Jonah's fault was the kind that fools a model and slips a threshold. So one root failure—the wrong mesh—was tailored, by accident, to slip through both backstops. The depth was an illusion. All three layers had the same blind spot, and the leak found it.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the non-spec grounding repair, the filtering of the anomalous reading, and the widened alert threshold, and that the three protective layers failed in sequence against a single fault.'

She stood.

'You built three walls,' she said. 'And the one leak you got was shaped exactly to pass through all three. He died on the other side of a defense that only looked like depth.'

She left the three records on the table.
