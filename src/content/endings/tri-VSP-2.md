No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a narrowed margin, a side agreement, and a reused part.

'Three ways to save a little,' she said. 'Elias—you halved the safety margin, calling the original timid. Priya—you'd signed a delivery commitment the science couldn't meet, so you needed every run to count. Jonah—you reused a single-use part to save the budget. Three economies. One body.'

The three were silent.

'They rhyme,' Ellery said. 'Elias, you economized on margin—cut the cushion to gain throughput. Priya, you economized on truth—promised more than existed to gain a deal. Jonah, you economized on parts—reused what should be discarded to gain a few dollars. Each of you spent a little safety to buy a little something. And the somethings stacked. A thin margin, a hard deadline, a worn part—all leaning the same way, toward the run, away from the man.'

'The margin was poorly justified,' Varga said.

'The commitment was standard,' Priya said.

'The part looked fine,' Jonah said.

'You all believed your economy was harmless,' Ellery said. 'And separately, each nearly was. A halved margin with good parts and an honest timeline—survivable. A worn part with a full margin—probably caught. An aggressive deadline with proper margins and parts—just stressful. But you didn't get one economy. You got three, compounding. The worn part fails into the half-margin that Elias removed, on a run that Priya's deadline wouldn't let anyone reconsider.'

The room was very quiet.

'There's a word for the thing the three of you served,' Ellery said. 'Efficiency. Doing more with less. It's a virtue in this building—Priya preaches it, Elias prides himself on it, Jonah was trying to practice it. And efficiency is just the systematic removal of slack. You each removed slack from your own domain. And slack is the same thing as safety margin. It's what's there for the bad day. You spent all of it, three different ways, and the bad day came with nothing held in reserve.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the halved safety margin, the undisclosed delivery commitment, and the reused single-use part, and that together they eliminated the system's reserve against failure.'

She stood.

'You were all being efficient,' she said. 'Lean. Disciplined. And lean means nothing left over for the moment it goes wrong. It went wrong, and there was nothing left over, because the three of you had spent it.'

She left the three records on the table.
