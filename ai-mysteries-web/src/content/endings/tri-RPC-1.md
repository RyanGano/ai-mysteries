No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a dark alarm zone, a bad part, and a widened threshold.

'Marcus—you left the Charge Cage sub-zone disarmed, so nothing watched the door. Jonah—you repaired the grounding mesh with non-spec material, so a fault could find a path. Renée—you widened the alert threshold, so the building current didn't escalate.' She set them down. 'Nothing watched. The leak was made. The alarm was raised out of reach.'

The three were silent.

'Three layers of watching, all dimmed,' Ellery said. 'The motion sensor that watches the door—Marcus, dark. The grounding mesh that watches for stray current physically, by shunting it safely—Jonah, substituted. The monitoring threshold that watches for stray current electronically—Renée, raised. The physical guard and both the human and electronic eyes, all reduced at once.'

'I forgot the re-arm,' Rourke said.

'I was out of the spec mesh,' Jonah said.

'I retuned to cut false alarms,' Renée said.

'Each of you dimmed a different watch,' Ellery said. 'And the thing about watches is they're meant to overlap. If the mesh fails, the monitor catches the current. If the monitor's tuned down, the door sensor at least catches the person entering an active fault. Three overlapping guards, so no single gap is fatal. Except all three had a gap, in the same place, at the same time. The overlap was gone.'

The room was very quiet.

'Defense in depth only works if the layers are independent and intact,' Ellery said. 'Marcus, your dark zone meant no one was alerted that a man had entered. Jonah, your bad mesh meant the fault had somewhere to go. Renée, your raised threshold meant the growing current rang no bell. A man entered unwatched, a fault leaked unshunted, and the alarm that should have caught it had been moved past where it would fire. Three dimmed watches, one dark room.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the un-rearmed sub-zone, the non-spec grounding repair, and the widened detection threshold, and that the three failures eliminated the overlapping protection the bay was designed to have.'

She stood.

'Three guards,' she said. 'A sensor, a mesh, a threshold. Each of you turned one down. He stood in the one spot all three were looking away from.'

She left the three records on the table.
