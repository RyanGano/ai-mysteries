No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a compressed schedule and a skipped verification.

'Priya,' she said, 'you cut the maintenance window from ninety minutes to forty.' She turned. 'Jonah, in those forty minutes, you skipped the post-swap verification.' She set the two records down. 'You took the time. He took the shortcut the missing time forced.'

'I gave a constraint,' Priya said. 'He confirmed he could meet it.'

'Did he?' Ellery looked at Jonah. 'Did you tell her you could do ninety minutes of work in forty, or did you tell her you'd try?'

Jonah looked at the table. 'I said I'd make it work,' he said quietly.

'"I'll make it work,"' Ellery repeated. 'That's the phrase that ties you two together. Because you two have a particular arrangement, don't you. Priya, you protect Jonah. You've shielded him from write-ups, kept him employed, stood between him and Michael when Michael wanted him gone. And Jonah, in return—' she paused. 'In return, when you hand him an impossible window, he makes it work. He doesn't say no. He never says no to you. That's the deal.'

The room was very still.

'It's not a corrupt deal,' Ellery said. 'It's a human one. Priya, you genuinely look out for him—I believe that. Jonah, you're genuinely loyal—I believe that too. But look at what the loyalty does. You'd say no to anyone else. If Elias handed you forty minutes for a ninety-minute job, you might push back, flag it, protect yourself. But Priya? Priya protects you. You can't let Priya down. So you cut the corner she needs cut, and you don't even make her ask.'

'I never told him to skip a safety step,' Priya said. 'Never. I would never.'

'I know,' Ellery said. 'You didn't have to. That's what the relationship is for. You set a number that could only be met by skipping a step, and you trusted Jonah to skip it without being told, because Jonah always finds a way to give you what you need. You outsourced the corner-cutting to his loyalty. He took the instruction you never gave.'

'She looks out for me,' Jonah said. It came out almost pleading. 'She's the only one who ever did. I wasn't going to be the reason her test didn't run.'

'And there it is,' Ellery said gently. 'You protected her test. She protects your job. And the thing in the middle of that lovely, mutual loyalty was a verification step on a suit a man was about to wear. You'd built a relationship that turns her deadlines into his shortcuts, automatically, without a word. It served you both for years. It killed him once.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the compressed window, the skipped verification, and the established protective relationship between the two of you that made the second a predictable consequence of the first.'

She stood.

'Look after each other a little less,' she said. 'It's a strange thing to ask. But your loyalty has a body count now.'

She left the schedule and the checklist on the table, touching.
