No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a deferred recalibration, a compressed window, and a skipped check.

'This one's a straight line,' she said. 'Elias—you deferred the recalibration, so the sensors ran drifted. Priya—you compressed the maintenance window and flagged it must-run. Jonah—in the window you had left, you skipped the post-swap verification.' She set them down in order. 'Drifted sensors. No time. No check.'

The three were silent.

'Watch how it hands off,' Ellery said. 'Elias's deferral means the suit needs careful verification—it's running on stale calibration, exactly the situation where you check twice. Priya's compressed window means there's no time to verify carefully. Jonah, caught between a suit that needed extra checking and a clock that allowed none, skips the check.' She paused. 'Each decision made the next one's failure inevitable. The drift created the need. The schedule removed the time. The technician dropped the step. One, two, three.'

'I deferred within the manual,' Varga said.

'I set a schedule within my authority,' Priya said.

'I did what I could in the time,' Jonah said.

'Here's the cruelty of it,' Ellery said. 'Elias, your deferral would have been fine if Jonah had verified—drifted sensors get caught in a careful check. Priya, your schedule would have been fine if the suit hadn't been running drifted—a quick check is enough for a calibrated suit. Jonah, your skipped check would have been fine if either of the other two conditions hadn't held. Three decisions, and each one is only fatal in the presence of the other two.'

The room was very quiet.

'And there's a current that runs through all three of you,' Ellery said. 'Pressure. Elias deferred to protect the test window. Priya compressed to hit the date. Jonah cut the check to make Priya's number. The same force—the schedule, the milestone, the thing that wouldn't bend—pushed all three of your hands. You weren't conspiring. You were all leaning into the same wind.'

'The report,' she said, 'will lay out the chain: deferred recalibration, compressed window, skipped verification. It will note that each handoff made the next failure more likely, and that all three traced back to schedule pressure.'

She stood.

'A drifted suit, no time, no check,' she said. 'Hand to hand to hand. He put on the suit at the end of the line.'

She left the three records in their row.
