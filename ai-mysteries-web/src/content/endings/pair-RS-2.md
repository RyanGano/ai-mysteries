No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a deleted visitor record and a press release draft.

'Marcus,' she said, 'after the incident you deleted the investor's sign-in. Made the audience disappear.' She turned. 'Priya, seventy-six minutes after the death, you wrote a statement announcing the program would continue on schedule.' She set them down. 'One of you erased the reason for the rush. One of you confirmed the rush would go on. Two cover-ups, pointing opposite directions, hiding the same thing.'

'I was protecting the company,' Rourke said.

'I was protecting the company,' Priya said—and then heard herself, and stopped.

'You used the same words,' Ellery said. 'Independently. Within a breath of each other. That's what I keep noticing about you two. You don't coordinate. You don't need to. You both have the same instinct, and the instinct is: protect the institution, manage what's seen.' She paused. 'Marcus removes the witness. Priya writes the narrative. One subtracts the inconvenient fact, one adds the reassuring story. Between you, the morning gets edited into something survivable for the company before the man is even cold.'

The room was quiet.

'Here's what your two instincts add up to,' Ellery said. 'Marcus, you erase the investor—so no one can ask why the demo couldn't wait. Priya, you announce continuity—so no one thinks to stop and examine the demo at all. Subtract the question, supply the answer. It's a complete operation. And you each did only your half, on your own, because it's just what you do.'

'Communicating in a crisis isn't a crime,' Priya said.

'Cleaning up a log isn't a crime,' Rourke said.

'Apart, they're barely anything,' Ellery agreed. 'A deleted entry. A fast statement. But together they're a machine for making sure no one ever asks the question that matters: why did this test, with these problems, have to run this morning? Marcus took away the evidence of the pressure. Priya took away the pause where someone might feel it. You built, between you, without a word, a perfect little system for keeping the rush invisible.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the deletion of the visitor record and the timing and content of the public statement, that both occurred within hours of the death, and that together they obscured the commercial pressure behind the test.'

She stood.

'You're the same animal,' she said. 'A protector and an operator, and you both protect the building first and the truth never. You didn't conspire. You're just two versions of the same reflex, and the reflex, doubled, erased a man's last morning before anyone could read it.'

She left the deleted record and the draft on the table, two halves of the same instinct.
