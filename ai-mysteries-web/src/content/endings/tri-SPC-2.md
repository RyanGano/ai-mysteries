No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a downgraded near-miss, a reused part, and a closed safety case.

'Three weeks ago the fault fired in an empty bay,' she said. 'Priya—you downgraded the near-miss to "monitor." Renée—you closed the safety case without a root cause. Jonah—the part that failed both times was a single-use gasket you'd reused to save the budget.' She set them down. 'The warning came, and the two of you who should have acted on it filed it away, while the cause of it sat in the suit the whole time.'

The three were silent.

'The building gave you a free one,' Ellery said. 'A full rehearsal—the exact fault, no one inside. That's a gift. And what did the three of you do with the gift? Jonah, you'd caused it with a reused part, and didn't know, because you'd never logged the reuse. Priya, you downgraded it because acting would cost time and money. Renée, you closed it because you were tired and a fight with Priya over an unmanned event wasn't worth it. The cause was hidden, the severity was lowered, the case was shut.'

'A single event isn't a pattern,' Priya said.

'I dispositioned it per standard,' Renée said.

'The parts looked fine,' Jonah said.

'Here's the loop,' Ellery said. 'Jonah, your reuse—done to honor the cost discipline Priya preaches—caused the first fault. Priya, your cost-and-schedule priorities led you to downgrade it. Renée, the same pressures that wore you thin led you to close it. The fault was a symptom of the exact culture that then dismissed the symptom. The disease diagnosed itself and the three of you prescribed ignoring it.'

The room was very quiet.

'You had three weeks,' Ellery said. 'Three weeks between the warning and the death. In those three weeks, any of you could have reopened it. Jonah, you could have come clean about the reused parts. Priya, you could have upgraded the near-miss when nothing explained it. Renée, you could have refused to let an unexplained fault stay closed. Three weeks, three people, three chances a day. The gift the building gave you sat unopened until the morning the price came due.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the downgraded near-miss, the closed safety case, and the reused single-use part, and that the prior unmanned event constituted a clear three-week warning that was not acted upon by any of the three responsible.'

She stood.

'It showed you exactly how he'd die,' she said. 'Three weeks early, for free, with the bay empty. You buried the cause, lowered the alarm, and shut the file. Then you ran it for real.'

She left the three records on the table.
