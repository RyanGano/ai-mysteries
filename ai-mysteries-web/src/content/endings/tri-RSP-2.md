No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a suppressed alert, a downgraded near-miss, and a bypass jumper.

'Three weeks of warnings, all silenced,' she said. 'Marcus—you suppressed the proximity alert. Priya—you downgraded the near-miss that fired the same fault in an empty bay. Jonah—you left a bypass jumper across the interlock.' She set them down. 'The room couldn't warn. The history was buried. The suit couldn't halt.'

The three were silent.

'The building tried to tell you,' Ellery said. 'Three weeks ago, the fault fired in an empty bay—a free warning, no one hurt. Priya, you marked it "monitor." Then the proximity alert that would have flagged a person in the bay—Marcus, you'd muted it as a nuisance. Then the interlock that would have stopped the discharge—Jonah, your jumper bridged it. The warning was buried, the eyes were closed, the brake was cut.'

'A single unmanned event isn't a pattern,' Priya said.

'The alert cried wolf constantly,' Rourke said.

'I forgot to pull the jumper,' Jonah said.

'Each of you silenced a different alarm,' Ellery said. 'Priya, the historical one—the near-miss that said this will happen again. Marcus, the spatial one—the alert that said someone's here now. Jonah, the immediate one—the interlock that said stop this instant. Past, present, and the final second. Three alarms, three silences. The system screamed three times, in three registers, and not one of them was audible.'

The room was very quiet.

'If the near-miss had stayed live,' Ellery said, 'the fault is investigated and fixed before the morning. If the proximity alert had been active, someone hauls him out. If the jumper had been pulled, the interlock halts. Three independent saves, each defeated by a different one of you, none of you knowing the others had defeated theirs.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the suppressed proximity alert, the downgraded near-miss, and the installed bypass jumper, and that each silenced a distinct warning of the same fault.'

She stood.

'It warned you three times,' she said. 'Weeks ahead, in the room, and at the last second. Between the three of you, every warning was off. He died in a building that had been shouting.'

She left the three records on the table.
