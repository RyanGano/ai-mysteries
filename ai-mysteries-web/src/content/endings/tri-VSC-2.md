No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a calibration deferral, a must-run flag, and an un-invoked halt.

'Three positions on one decision,' she said. 'Should this test run? Elias—you deferred the recalibration, knowing the suit would run drifted, and said nothing. Priya—you flagged it must-run, removing the question. Renée—you had the halt authority and the doubt, and you stayed in your chair.' She set them down. 'The person who knew it was risky stayed quiet. The person who could force it forced it. The person who could stop it didn't.'

The three were silent.

'It's almost a perfect division of failure,' Ellery said. 'Elias, you had the knowledge—you knew the drift made it dangerous. Priya, you had the power—you made it unstoppable. Renée, you had the authority—and didn't use it. Knowledge that stayed silent. Power that pushed. Authority that abstained. Three different relationships to the same go/no-go, all of them resolving toward go.'

'I deferred maintenance within the manual,' Varga said.

'I prioritized within my authority,' Priya said.

'I had been overruled before,' Renée said.

'Here's the thing,' Ellery said. 'Renée, you'll say a halt wouldn't have stuck against Priya's flag. Maybe. But Elias knew the specific reason it was dangerous that morning—the drift. If Elias had spoken, you'd have had grounds even Priya couldn't reframe. And if you'd halted, Elias's quiet knowledge would have had to come out. Each of your failures protected the others. Elias's silence meant Renée had no ammunition. Renée's abstention meant Elias's silence was never tested. Priya's flag meant neither of them felt they could move.'

The room was very quiet.

'You three are the test's three checkpoints,' Ellery said. 'Technical judgment—Elias. Operational authority—Priya. Safety authority—Renée. A test that passes all three is supposed to be safe. This one passed all three: Elias didn't object, Priya approved, Renée didn't halt. Three green lights. Every one of them wrong, each for its own reason.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the deferred recalibration left unflagged, the must-run designation, and the un-invoked halt authority, and that the three checkpoints meant to gate the test all cleared it.'

She stood.

'The one who knew didn't say. The one who could stop it didn't. And the one in between made sure it couldn't be stopped,' she said. 'Three checkpoints, three failures, one green light made of three.'

She left the three records on the table.
