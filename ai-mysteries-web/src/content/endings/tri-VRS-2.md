No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had three records of things made to disappear.

'After Michael died,' she said, 'three records were altered or absent. Elias—the telemetry, four hundred milliseconds out of sync, unreconstructable. Marcus—the camera footage, gone to a manual storage cycle. Priya—the incident summary, curated before it reached the board.' She set them down. 'No reliable data. No video. No honest summary. Between the three of you, there's almost nothing left to see the morning by.'

'The sync was an optimization,' Varga said.

'The storage cycle was routine,' Rourke said.

'The summary was distilled for the board,' Priya said.

'Three innocent explanations,' Ellery said. 'And between them, a morning that's nearly invisible. I had three ways to reconstruct what happened—the numbers, the pictures, the official account. Each of you compromised one. Not together. You don't even like each other. But the result is an event that resists every standard method of investigation, because the data's unreliable, the video's gone, and the summary's been shaped.'

The room was quiet.

'I want to be careful,' Ellery said. 'I'm not saying you three agreed to bury this. I'm saying you each have a habit. Elias trusts his model over the mess and degrades the mess. Marcus leaves no record. Priya curates the narrative. Those habits are who you are. And on the one morning it mattered, your three habits combined to erase the event almost completely. Habit doesn't need a meeting.'

'Then you have nothing,' Priya said.

'I have the erasures,' Ellery said. 'That's the thing. When the data, the video, and the summary are all compromised by three different people in three different ways, the pattern of erasure becomes the evidence. I can't see the morning. But I can see three people who each, by reflex, removed their kind of proof. And three reflexes pointing the same way, on the same day, tells me more than any single clean record would have.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the degraded telemetry, the absent footage, and the curated summary, the separate cause of each, and that together they rendered the incident largely unreconstructable.'

She stood.

'You each took away one way of seeing,' she said. 'The numbers, the pictures, the words. He died in the dark the three of you made.'

She left the three compromised records on the table.
