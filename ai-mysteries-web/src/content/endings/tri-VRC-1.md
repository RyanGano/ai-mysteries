No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had three threshold-and-access records.

'Three lines,' she said. 'Elias—you narrowed the discharge threshold, so the fault read as safe. Renée—you widened the alert threshold, so the reading didn't escalate. Marcus—you suppressed the presence layer, so the room didn't see the man.' She set them down. 'Two lines moved, one eye closed. A fault that's not dangerous, an alarm that doesn't fire, a man who isn't there.'

The three were silent.

'You're the two careful ones and the careful one,' Ellery said, and almost smiled at how it sounded. 'Elias and Renée, you've both spent years warning about recklessness. Marcus, you've spent years keeping this place secure. The three people most invested in nothing going wrong. And between you, you tuned the danger line down, tuned the alarm line up, and turned the eyes off.'

'My threshold corrected an over-conservative margin,' Varga said.

'Mine reduced nuisance escalations,' Renée said.

'My flag was routine maintenance,' Rourke said.

'Each defensible,' Ellery said. 'And here's what they make together. The fault fires. Elias's threshold says: not dangerous. Renée's threshold says: not worth an alarm. Marcus's flag says: no one's here anyway. Three reassurances, from three careful people, all wrong at once, all pointing at the same false conclusion—nothing is happening.'

The room was very quiet.

'This is the one that frightens me most,' Ellery said. 'Not the careless people. The careful ones. Elias and Renée each trusted the other's caution to be the backstop, and trimmed their own. Marcus trusted that the engineering and the safety systems would catch anything his flag let through. Three people leaning on each other's diligence, each becoming a little less diligent because surely the others have it covered.'

'The report,' she said, 'will note the narrowed discharge threshold, the widened alert threshold, and the suppressed presence layer, that each was independently defensible, and that together they produced a false all-clear.'

She stood.

'The three of you would have died to prevent this,' she said. 'I believe that. And you caused it, between you, by each trusting the other two to be the careful one. He fell through the floor of your shared confidence.'

She left the three records on the table.
