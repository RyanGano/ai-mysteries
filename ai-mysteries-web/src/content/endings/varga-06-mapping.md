No one asked why they were there this time.
The meeting had been called without an agenda, without a subject line, without the small procedural courtesies that usually softened bad news. That alone told them this wasn't another reconstruction.

Detective Mara Ellery had a wiring diagram.

'Sensor channel seven,' she said. 'It used to map to the left shoulder presence pad. After your revision, it maps to the chest plate.' She looked at Elias Varga. 'You called the original a labeling error.'

'It was a labeling error,' Varga said. 'The schematic from the fabricator was inconsistent with the firmware. One of them was wrong. I made them consistent.'

'You made them consistent with each other,' Ellery said. 'You didn't check which one was right.'

A pause.

'I checked the schematic,' Varga said. 'The schematic is the canonical reference. The firmware is downstream. When two sources disagree, you defer to the authoritative one. That is not negligence. That is method.'

'The fabricator's schematic had the error,' Ellery said. 'Jonah Pike will tell you. The pads were physically wired the way the firmware described. You changed the firmware to match a drawing that was wrong.'

Varga's face did not move, but something behind it did.

'So when Michael Holloway leaned against the chest plate,' she said, 'the system read it as pressure on a pad that, as far as it knew, was on his shoulder. The geometry was off. The clearance calculation was off. By exactly the width of a man's torso.'

'You're describing a single sensor remap,' Varga said. 'One of several hundred I reviewed that quarter. The probability that any individual change—'

'You reviewed several hundred,' Ellery said. 'And you got them all right except the one that was downstream of a bad drawing. You trusted the document over the hardware. You always trust the document.'

He was quiet.

'That's not a flaw, exactly,' she said. 'It's how you've been right your whole life. The written record is more reliable than people's memories of what they wired. Usually it is.' She paused. 'This time the record was wrong and the people were right, and you couldn't hear it, because you don't listen to people. You read.'

The room held still.

'The report will note that sensor channel seven was remapped to resolve a documentation conflict. It will note that the resolution was incorrect. It will note that the physical configuration was never verified against the change.'

She rolled up the diagram.

'You corrected the firmware to agree with the drawing,' she said. 'And the drawing was the lie. Strictly speaking.'

She left.
