The kitchen was quiet when the inspector came in.

"Somebody moved the pie," she said, setting down her notebook. "And I want to know who."

The cook wiped his hands on his apron. "Not me. I was out back the whole time, scrubbing pots until my arms ached and the sun went down behind the barn. Ask anyone who walked past the yard that afternoon. They all saw me there, elbow deep in soap, and none of them saw a pie."
